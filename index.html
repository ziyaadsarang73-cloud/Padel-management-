Tournament Rules

  • Classic 3 Point: Each player teams up with every other player once. Points collected individually.
  • Team Classic 3 Point: Each team plays every other team once. Points collected per team.
  • Classic Americano: Each player teams up with every other player once. Points collected individually.
  • Mixed Americano: Players team with opposite sex once. Points collected individually.
  • Team Americano: Each team plays every other team once. Points collected per team.
