Tournament Rules

  • Classic 3 point: Each player teams up with every other player once. Points collected individually.
  • Team Classic 3 point: Teams play each other once. Points collected per team.
  • Classic Americano: Each player teams up with every other player once. Points collected individually.
  • Mixed Americano: Players team up with opposite sex once. Points collected individually.
  • Team Americano: Teams play each other once. Points collected per team.
  • Official Padel Tennis rules applied for scoring, court setup, and match play.
