PadelPilot Rules

Game Rules (Official)

  • Padel is played in doubles on a 10m x 20m enclosed court.
  • The net divides the court; serve must be made diagonally from one service box to another.
  • Serves must be underhand, below waist height, after the ball bounces once behind the baseline.
  • Balls may be played off walls on your side before crossing the net.
  • Ball that bounces twice, hits net incorrectly, or touches the fence on the wrong side results in point loss.

Scoring Rules

  • Points follow tennis scoring: 15, 30, 40, Advantage, Game.
  • Deuce (40-40) requires two consecutive points to win the game.
  • Sets: first to 6 games with 2-game lead. 6-6 triggers tiebreak.
  • Matches are best of 3 sets.
  • Service faults: serve above waist, wrong box, stepping over line.

Tournament & Randomizer Rules

Classic 3 Point

  • Each player teams up with every other player once.
  • Points collected individually.
  • Follows official scoring rules.

Team Classic 3 Point

  • Pre-defined teams play all other teams once.
  • Points are collected per team.
  • Standard padel scoring applies.

Classic Americano

  • Each player teams up with others once in Americano format.
  • Points collected individually.

Mixed Americano

  • Players team with a partner of opposite sex once.
  • Points collected individually.

Team Americano

  • Pre-defined teams play all other teams once.
  • Points collected per team.

Key Terms

  • Let: Serve touches net but lands correctly — replayed.
  • Fault: Serve violates official rules.
  • Volley: Ball hit before bouncing; legal except on serve.
